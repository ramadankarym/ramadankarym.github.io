Ramadan 1448 questions

When does Ramadan 1448 start?

Astronomical calculations expect the first night at sunset on Sunday 7 February 2027, and the first fasting day on Monday 8 February 2027. The official announcement depends on moon sighting, so it can differ by a day.

How many days are left until Ramadan?

The countdown at the top of the page counts days, hours, minutes and seconds until sunset on 7 February 2027, Cairo time. You can switch between first-night sunset and first-day Fajr.

Is the greeting video maker free?
